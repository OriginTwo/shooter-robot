THEME: EVERYTHING HAS A COST
- every action has an unintended side effect



* top-down shooter game
* you have a certain amount of time to prepare for invasion at night

* player is robot powered by solar cells, so energy is regenerated
	automatically during the day

* everything costs energy

* subsystems (access through simple ui):
	* power:
		* reroute power to different subsystems
		* 10 bars of power
		* more power in subsystem = better
			* more power in movement subsystem = faster movement
			* more power in weapon subsystem = faster shoot
			* more power in synthesizer subsystem = faster production
	* movement:
		* consume energy
	* weapon:
		* consume ammo
	* synthesizer:
		* consume energy
		* produce consumables:
			* ammo
			* parts    (health)
			* battery  (energy)
